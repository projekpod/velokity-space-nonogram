# Privasi Velokity: Space Nonogram

Progres puzzle, koin, album, kostum, dan pengaturan disimpan melalui SDK portal jika tersedia. Portal dapat menyinkronkan progres sesuai kebijakannya. Jika SDK tidak tersedia, progres disimpan di browser dan dapat hilang saat data situs dihapus atau browser diganti.

Pengembang Velokity tidak mengumpulkan data pribadi. Game tidak meminta akun, nama, atau alamat email. Iklan dan analitik, jika aktif, hanya berasal dari SDK portal tempat game dimainkan. Pemrosesan data oleh SDK mengikuti kebijakan privasi portal tersebut.

Tautan Traktir Velokity membuka layanan Trakteer. Kunjungan dan transaksi di sana mengikuti kebijakan privasi Trakteer.

## Privacy in English

Puzzle progress, coins, albums, outfits, and settings are saved through the hosting portal’s SDK when available. The portal may sync progress under its own policy. Without the SDK, progress stays in your browser and can be lost when you clear site data or change browsers.

The Velokity developer does not collect personal data. The game does not ask for an account, name, or email address. Ads and analytics, where enabled, come only from the hosting portal’s SDK and follow that portal’s privacy policy.

The optional support link opens Trakteer. Visits and transactions there follow Trakteer’s privacy policy.
