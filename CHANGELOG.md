# Changelog

## 1.1.0

- Reveal 300 pixel pictures across 15 worlds. Boards range from 5×5 to 15×15, with zoom and pan on larger boards.
- Play 365 daily Rasi Bintang puzzles.
- Try six level twists with illustrated tips. Start with revealed cells or locked asteroid cells, find a hidden bonus star, use a shield, race the comet timer for extra coins, or aim for a perfect run.
- Spend coins on cats, fur colors, and costumes.
- Build a fill streak to crack the board and see Velokity hold it together. The animated space backdrop adds meteors, comets, and twinkling stars.
- Music and sound effects are louder, with separate saved volume sliders and gentler effects in Calm Mode.

## 1.0.0

- The first release included 60 Nonograms across Moon, Mars, and Saturn, 120 daily Rasi Bintang puzzles, planet albums, costumes, and three-stage Bonus Rounds.
